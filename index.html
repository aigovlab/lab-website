About the lab

The AI Governance Lab studies how law, policy, and institutions can keep pace with rapidly advancing artificial intelligence. Our work spans regulatory design, international coordination, and the technical literacy that policymakers need to govern AI systems responsibly.

We work across computer science, law, and public policy, because the questions do: what a model can do is an engineering question, what it should be allowed to do is not. The lab produces research meant to be rigorous enough for the academy and usable by the people writing the rules.

Training is central to the lab. Students and postdoctoral researchers lead their own projects with regular mentorship, and the lab supports that work with data, funding, and close collaboration.
